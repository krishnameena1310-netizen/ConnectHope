05 / Find human support

Friendship takes time. Consider reaching out to a trusted family member, teacher, counsellor or someone you already know. A supervised hobby group or community activity can offer a low-pressure way to meet people.

If loneliness is persistent or affecting everyday life, a qualified mental health professional can help. In India, Tele-MANAS offers mental health support at 14416. In an immediate emergency, call 112.

ConnectHope is an educational project, not a counselling service, diagnosis tool or emergency service. It does not match strangers or collect contact details.

About the project

ConnectHope addresses social isolation by turning the broad goal of making friends into approachable, interest-based activities. It uses conditional logic for check-ins, arrays for conversation prompts, functions for interaction, loops for rendering and local storage for optional progress.
